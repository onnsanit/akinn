Foundation

Two colours, one temperature

Measured from your file, not approximated. Neither is neutral: the bone carries a faint green-warm bias and the ink carries the same one, which is why the pair feels calm instead of clinical — they agree about temperature. Never substitute pure white or pure black; both read colder than the brand.

Colour system

The asset palette sits on bone

The wealth-tree hues are mid-value on purpose, and bone flatters them: every tier reads clearly without any circle shouting. One rule keeps it accessible — these colours are fills, never text. On bone they land between 1.3:1 and 3.4:1, which is fine for a shape carrying a label and never fine for the label itself. Words are always Ink.

Wordmark

Rules that keep it composed

Clear space

Keep a margin of one n-width on every side of the wordmark. Nothing enters it — not the tree, not the tagline, not a photo edge.

Minimum size

The hairline serifs are the identity and they die first. Floor: 28 px tall on screen, 8 mm in print. Below that, use the ink dot from the i as a bug, or nothing.

Two colourways only

Ink on Bone, or Bone on Ink. The wordmark never takes an asset colour, never sits on a photograph without a bone panel behind it, and never gets a gradient.

With the tree

The tree diagram sits above, the wordmark below, gap equal to one stem height. The tree brings the colour; the wordmark stays ink. They never compete because they never share a register.

The mark

One tree, drawn once

The pictorial mark is the diagram at its smallest honest size: one stem, one fork, three holdings. It is fixed geometry — designed, not generated — so it is the same tree on every surface. Portfolio carries the crown, equity beside it, gold as the small bright one; the ink-only version is the same drawing for stamps, embossing and one-colour print.

Typeface

Name the face before anything ships

This page sets the wordmark in Bodoni Moda as a stand-in, and it is visibly not your logo: your face has softer, flared stems and warmer contrast — the family of Saol Display, Canela or Ogg (all commercial). If the logo came from a designer, ask them for the typeface name and licence; if it came from a generator, we should redraw the five letters as outlines so the mark is yours regardless of any font licence. Five letters is a small, worthwhile job.

HaveA raster image of the wordmark. Enough to measure colour, not enough to reproduce cleanly at scale.
NeedThe vector (SVG/PDF/AI) or the typeface name — either unlocks favicons, print, and the app icon without redrawing.
Free stand-insBodoni Moda (closest contrast) or Fraunces (closest warmth) — for mockups only, never for the shipped mark.
